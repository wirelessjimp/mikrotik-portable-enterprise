# Advanced Labs

Day 2 ends with advanced labs of your pick. They come from the full guide and were adapted for your kit. Two things to know.

- **They haven't been rewalked on a prebuilt kit.** If a step doesn't match what you see on your screen, tell the instructor.
- **Your kit already has what these labs used to build.** The instructor's script set up the graphs, the time servers, and the static DNS entries, so these labs have you look at them and then change them.

| Lab | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Lab 20 — Graphing and Bandwidth Test](lab-20.md) | Interface graphs, and a bandwidth test to a public server and between your two devices |
| [Lab 21 — Time and NTP](lab-21.md) | Your NTP client and server, and handing out your L009 as the time server |
| [Lab 22 — DNS](lab-22.md) | Static entries, upstream servers, DNS over HTTPS, forcing DNS through your router, and DNS adblock |

Looking for how to change an identity or a password, upgrade RouterOS, or schedule a reboot? That is [Appendix F — Administrative Cheat Sheet](appendix-f.md).
