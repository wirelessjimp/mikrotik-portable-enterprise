# Advanced Labs

Day 2 ends with advanced labs of your pick. They come from the full guide and were written for building a router from an empty device. Two things follow from that.

- **Prerequisite numbers belong to the full guide, not to this one.** When a lab says it needs VLANs, DHCP, DNS, or the firewall, your kit already has them. The instructor's script built them before class.
- **They haven't been rewalked on a prebuilt kit.** If a step doesn't match what you see on your screen, tell the instructor.

| Lab | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Lab 21 — Traffic Analysis](lab-21.md) | Packet sniffer and Wireshark, live streaming to Wireshark, Torch |
| [Lab 22 — Useful Tools](lab-22.md) | TFTP, FTP, and interface graphing. TFTP and FTP are also in Lab 14. |
| [Lab 24 — Time & NTP](lab-24.md) | System clock, NTP client, NTP server |
| [Lab 25 — WAN Sources](lab-25.md) | USB tethering, a USB cellular modem, Wi-Fi client mode as a WAN. Wi-Fi as a WAN is also in Lab 12. |
| [Lab 28 — DNS Advanced](lab-28.md) | Static entries, upstream servers, DNS over HTTPS |
| [Lab 29 — Administrative Tasks](lab-29.md) | Identity, password, RouterOS and RouterBOARD upgrades, users, scheduled reboot, logs |
