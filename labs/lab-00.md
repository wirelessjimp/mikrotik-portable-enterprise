# Lab 0 — Open Your Tools

*Prerequisites: You've joined the class Wi-Fi and opened `http://portal.lab` (see the slide at the front of the room).*

**Why:** You'll use two pages all class, the lab guide and your Lab Notes sheet. Open both now and leave them open in their own tabs.

1. On the portal, click **Lab Guide**. It opens in a new tab. Leave it open and keep following the steps from there.
2. Back on the portal tab, click **Lab Notes**. It also opens in a new tab.
3. Record each password and credential you create in Lab Notes as you go.
4. Click **Save to File**. Look near the address bar for a download pop-up that says **Insecure download blocked**, and click **Keep**. Do this after each lab that adds a credential.
5. Open the saved file from your **Downloads** folder and use that copy for the rest of class.

> ### ⚠️ STOP AND READ
> Lab Notes does not store anything. If you refresh the page or close the tab, everything you typed is gone. Only a saved file keeps your entries.

> ### ⚠️ STOP AND READ
> Your browser may block the download. The pop-up disappears after a few seconds, and the button says **Saved!** either way, but **your notes aren't saved until you click Keep**. Once your laptop is plugged into your router, the portal may stop loading by name, so save your notes before you plug in.

> **Note:** After the first save, your copy opens from your own laptop, so saving from it isn't blocked. Each save creates a new numbered copy, such as `my-lab-notes (1).html`. Open the newest one.

> ### 🔐 Use Throwaway Passwords
> This is a classroom. **Don't reuse any password from your real life here.** Simple ones like `Testing1234` or `Cisco1234` are fine. Your saved notes file isn't encrypted, so treat everything in it as readable by anyone who opens it.
